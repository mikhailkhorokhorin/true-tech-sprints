# Task 1. Reply to a hostile client email in three tones

**Type:** Text · **Points:** 2 · **Answer limit:** 5000 characters · **Attempts:** unlimited

## Statement

A prompt engineer received an email from a hostile client. Prepare three reply options: neutral, diplomatic and confident. Choose the best one for this situation and explain your choice.

> To: Prompt engineer | Cc: his manager
>
> Subject: RE: "AI scoring" project status – URGENT
>
> Good afternoon!
>
> Let me say straight away that I am extremely unhappy with how this project is going. The pilot launch was agreed for July 3. Today is the 27th and there is neither a pilot nor any information about a change of dates. At the steering committee you said "almost everything is ready". The marketing campaign is ready, we are losing money every day.
>
> I ask you to provide the reasons for the delay, a realistic date, and a meeting with your manager. If I do not get an answer by the end of the week, I will escalate.

**Context (not for the client):**

- The delay is real: the security team requires an additional audit under Russian law.
- A realistic date is August 5.
- At the steering committee you were talking about the code, but did not account for approvals.
- Your manager is aware of the situation.
